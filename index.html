The 2-Hour Traffic Problem

The Driver's Loss

When traffic hits a standstill, standard ride fares freeze, but fuel keeps burning. To make it worse, traditional apps still extract a 30% fee. Drivers are actively losing money while trapped in jams.

The Passenger's Burnout

Professionals are exhausted. Sitting in cramped spaces causes blood sugar drops, BP spikes, and motion sickness from staring down at phones. A 90-minute commute becomes a total waste of highly skilled human time.

Our Three Pillars of Profit

How we detach the passenger from the stress and monetize the ride.

1. Absolute Relaxation

We combat the physical stress of traffic. Cabs are equipped with 12V Pay-Per-Minute Foot Massagers under the seat, and a low-power cooling pouch stocking chilled ORS/Water.

Win-Win: Drivers earn immediate retail margins on the water and a 60% cut of the massager fee, easily paying for their idle fuel.

2. Captive Entertainment

We get passengers off their phones. The eye-level headrest tablet offers offline E-books, comics, and interactive 3D product showrooms. Because it's an interior screen, it bypasses all government outdoor ad restrictions.

Monetization: Brands pay premium CPM for 3D interactive ads because the passenger is highly engaged for an entire hour.

3. Talent Royalty ("Hello Talented")

For top professionals, time is money. Pre-book a cab, select your skill (e.g., Coding, Math), and our platform pushes B2B micro-tasks to the tablet during the jam. Record a formula shortcut or review a layout on the fly.

The Royalty Loop: Corporate clients pay for the task. The earnings instantly pay off the passenger's cab fare and add royalty cash to their wallet.
